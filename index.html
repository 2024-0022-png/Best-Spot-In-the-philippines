About Siargao

Siargao is an island located in the province of Surigao del Norte. It has become one of the most popular tourist destinations in the Philippines.

The island is especially known for surfing, beautiful beaches, island hopping, lagoons, and its natural tropical scenery.

Why Visit Siargao?

Siargao offers a combination of adventure and relaxation. Visitors can explore islands, enjoy the beach, experience local culture, and appreciate the natural beauty of the area.

Beautiful Spots in Siargao

Cloud 9

Cloud 9 is Siargao's famous surfing destination. It is also known for its wooden boardwalk and beautiful ocean views.

Naked Island

Naked Island is a small sandbar surrounded by clear water. It is a popular stop during island-hopping tours.

Daku Island

Daku Island features a beautiful sandy beach, clear water, and relaxing tropical scenery.

Guyam Island

Guyam Island is a small tropical island with palm trees, white sand, and peaceful surroundings.
